## Product Description (Copy & Paste for Product Hunt)

Most online PDF tools force you to upload sensitive legal documents, medical scans, and tax returns to undisclosed cloud servers—only to hit you with a **$12/month paywall** right at the download screen.

We built **PDFCraft** to fix this. It is a modern, privacy-first PDF utility suite that runs **100% inside your local browser memory**. Your files never leave your device.

### 🌟 Key Superpowers:
* 🔒 **Military-Grade Irreversible Redaction:** Permanently purges underlying text glyphs, metadata, and `/Annots` objects.
* 📦 **Government Target Squeezer:** Compress PDFs strictly under **50KB, 100KB, 200KB, 300KB, or 500KB** using iterative binary-search quantization.
* ✍️ **Touch Signing via Real-Time WebRTC P2P:** Scan a QR code with your phone and sign with your finger. Your signature transfers directly device-to-device with zero intermediate servers.
* ✂️ **Dedicated Visual Split & Merge:** Reorder, rotate, delete, or split every N pages into a downloadable ZIP archive.
* 🛡️ **AES-256 Web Crypto PDF Lock/Unlock:** Encrypt and decrypt standard Adobe-compatible PDFs in-memory.
* ⚡ **Zero Signups, Zero Daily Limits:** Completely free, open source, and works offline in airplane mode.

## First Comment by Maker (Post immediately after launch)

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
> 
> I'm Vishnu, the creator of PDFCraft.
> 
> Earlier this month, I had to upload a signed certificate to a government portal that rejected anything over 100 KB. Every online PDF tool I tried wanted my email, my credit card, or gave me a 125 KB file that still failed. Even worse, they were uploading my sensitive personal files to unknown cloud servers.
> 
> So I asked a simple question: **With modern WebAssembly, Web Workers, and HTML5 Canvas, why does PDF editing still need a remote server?**
> 
> We engineered PDFCraft to do everything directly inside the user's browser:
> 1. Zero server uploads: Open your browser's DevTools Network tab—zero bytes are sent to any backend.
> 2. Real WebRTC touch signing: Point your phone camera at the screen, sign with your finger, and watch it appear on your laptop via a direct P2P DataChannel.
> 3. True irreversible redaction: Actually purges the underlying PDF text streams instead of just placing a black box on top.
>
